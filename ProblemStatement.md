PROBLEM STATEMENT:

.The recruitment process is often time-consuming and inefficient for both job seekers and employers.Students face difficulty
in finding suitable jobs, while employers struggle to manage job postings and applications effectively.

.This project proposes an AI-Based Job Portal with Employer Dashboard, where students can create profiles, upload resumes, 
search and apply for jobs, and receive skill-based job recommendations. Employers can post jobs, manage applicants, and
track the recruitment process through a centralized dashboard, making hiring faster and more efficient.
